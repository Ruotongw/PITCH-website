We introduce Pitch (Proactive Intervention Timing in CHat), a dataset of 1,091 human judgments of proactive agent timing collected from participants in 35 unscripted multi-party conversations they took part in. Using Pitch as a benchmark, we find that while models can place contributions well when given the entire conversation history, they systematically chime in too early when asked to decide live as the conversation unfolds.

Findings

Model performance drops sharply when deciding turn by turn, worse than lay humans

With the full transcript, 57.8% of model placements match a participant's ideal turn and 92.7% fall inside someone's acceptable window. Deciding live, turn by turn, these drop to 19.0% and 50.5%. Independent lay annotators doing the same turn-by-turn replay matched an ideal turn 23.7% of the time (models: 19.0%) and were less often premature (39.7% vs. 46.4%).

Models chime in too early

In turn-by-turn replay, 46.4% of placements come before every participant's acceptable window opens.
